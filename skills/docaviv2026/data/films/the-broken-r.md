## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
An intimate documentary essay that follows Ricardo as he returns to his parents' home and to the silence that always reigned around the kitchen table. Like his father, he too lives with Treacher Collins syndrome, and for years this genetic disorder marked him not only externally but also kept him from pronouncing the letter "R." Silence became a necessity, but suddenly, in his twenties, he decides to learn to speak. In his sensitive and captivating film, the creator and protagonist searches for the right word for the rejection that always pursued him, for his sexual orientation that wasn't spoken about in the family, and for the difficulty of accepting fate: why did he turn out this way? The virtuoso directorial language, which links narration, grainy VHS-tape footage and abstract nature images, expands the film beyond the personal story, as a universal experience of careful approach to a cracked and wounded past in order to move on.
