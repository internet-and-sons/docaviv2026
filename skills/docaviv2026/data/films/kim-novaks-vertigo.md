## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Without words, the beautiful face of a Hollywood actress fills the screen, a close-up of glory. Her seductive gaze and the black-and-white image conjure the "typecast" attached to Kim Novak: the femme fatale. After the bewitching opening, we cut to our time, to the cracked and fragile voice of Novak that testifies to the years that have passed. But Novak, who recently crossed 90, soon emerges as a sharp and brilliant woman, still enveloped in a natural glow. In intimate conversation with her, she opens boxes sealed for years and returns to the turbulent stations of her life: her admiration for Garbo and her friendship with Jimmy Stewart, the abuse by studio bosses and the guilt that still accompanies her, the reasons for leaving the spotlight in favor of a creative life in the wild nature of Oregon, and of course "Vertigo" is not absent from her chronicle. Similar to Hitchcock's masterpiece, this fascinating documentary also brings ghosts of the past to life, but they are ghosts Novak herself summons, in order to allow her and us to re-evaluate the woman who refused to break under the burden of her image.
