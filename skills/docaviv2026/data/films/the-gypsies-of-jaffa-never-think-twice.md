## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Jaffa, a city founded before the flood. A city of oranges, gypsies, English prostitutes, and Jews who came from the camps. There, in the camps, Tomas Voligora and Madame Zara the gypsy promised each other that if they survived, and if she had a son and the other a daughter, their children would marry. But things went wrong. Against the backdrop of the "Zingara" gypsy nightclub, in an atmosphere of spells, dances and cards, a gypsy and a dancing bear expose a story of love, betrayal and murder. "Never Think Twice," Ram Loevy's documentary, follows the dramatic and turbulent rehearsal process of the play "The Gypsies of Jaffa," at the Habima theater, all the way to the premiere. The film, produced in the early years of Israeli television, also deals with the theater's hidden struggle with the new medium.
