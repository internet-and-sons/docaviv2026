## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
An extraordinary documentation by Werner Herzog, who arrives in liberated Kuwait after the Gulf War and meets people and landscapes damaged in the war. Giant fires that were extinguished and human tragedies form a unique mosaic accompanied by a one-of-a-kind soundtrack. A different and entirely other view than the one we are used to seeing in the media, of the fields of destruction war leaves behind.
