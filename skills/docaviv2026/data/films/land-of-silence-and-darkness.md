## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
A special and one-of-a-kind glimpse into the world of the deaf-blind and the way they communicate with each other through the sense of touch. Herzog accompanies Fini Straubinger, who became deaf-blind at a young age and travels across Germany trying to help people in her condition. An observation of the isolated world of people who are outside society's boundaries of sight and hearing, and an experience of their lives that cannot be put into words.
