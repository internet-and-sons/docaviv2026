## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
The true and fascinating story of Aaron Sapiro, a young Jewish lawyer who made headlines in the 1920s when he set out on an unprecedented legal battle against one of the most powerful men in America: Henry Ford, industry magnate, automobile king and enthusiastic anti-Semite. Sapiro, who headed a farmers' organization, sued Ford over sharp defamation published in his newsletter, which spread mass conspiracy theories about a "gang of Jews" controlling the global economy and media. Within a public atmosphere loaded with hatred for anything that smelled of socialism or communism, Sapiro becomes an easy target. The film's careful examination of the trial in the spirit of a David and Goliath story raises the elusive connection between freedom of expression, power and racism — questions that continue to echo powerfully today.
