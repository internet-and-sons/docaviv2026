## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
"Follow Back" deals with intimacy, obsession and queer desire in public space. After discovering a live camera broadcasting from a stranger's home onto the street, the filmmaker moves to live under that man's balcony for a full month. Over the course of the month, a relationship is woven between them through physical and digital interactions, blurring the boundaries between public and private, virtual and physical, visible and hidden.
