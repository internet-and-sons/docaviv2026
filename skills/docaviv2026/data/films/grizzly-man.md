## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
For 13 years, Timothy Treadwell ventured out to a grizzly bear preserve in Alaska to live among and protect the bears. He filmed himself closely, and his honesty, idealism and naïveté captivated the public. Werner Herzog returns to the materials Treadwell shot and to the people who knew him to sketch a portrait of a classic Herzogian hero: obsessive, sensitive, on the brink of madness. A masterpiece providing direct observation of animals and humans as they are.
