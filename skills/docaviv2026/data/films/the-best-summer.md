## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Tamra Davis, a filmmaker whose past also includes directing dozens of music videos, one day found a box containing tapes from her old video camera. When she remembered what was recorded on them, she immediately realized it was a hidden treasure: raw, intense and intimate behind-the-scenes documentation of a legendary mid-1990s tour, where the giants of Gen X shared one stage at the height of their power: the Beastie Boys, Sonic Youth, Pavement, Beck, Rancid and the Foo Fighters. Without narration, present-day flashes, polish or too much explanation, viewers are hurled straight into the noise of the burning stage facing an inflamed audience and to the backstage, where Davis — accompanied by Kathleen Hanna, the Bikini Kill frontwoman serving as improvised interviewer — enjoyed free and direct access to the musicians, allowing us to see them in their most human and released moments, confessing about insecurity in front of crowds, about the differences between stage persona and reality and about musical influences. The rare time capsule manages to convey the raw energy of the period, and to smell exactly like teen spirit.
