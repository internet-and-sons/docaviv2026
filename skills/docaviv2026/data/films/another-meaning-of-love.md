## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
After many attempts to repair their relationship, Katy faces divorce for the second time in her life. Her son, the filmmaker, tries to understand together with her what really led there. Past traumas surface as the same question echoes in the background — what keeps people together, and what drives them apart?
