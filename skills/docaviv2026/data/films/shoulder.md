## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Independence Day 2008. Bitkhan, a 13-year-old girl, stands on the side of the road begging for help. In the silent car beside her lies her unconscious father with three small children around him. Many vehicles pass indifferently, until one Druze man stops, performs CPR and disappears without a trace. The father did not survive, and since then the family has been locked in suffocating silence for 17 years. The protagonist decides to break the silence and sets out on a journey to find the man, certain that he is the key to healing.
