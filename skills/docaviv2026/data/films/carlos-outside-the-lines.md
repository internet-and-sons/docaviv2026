## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Carlos from Kiryat Gat is the best children's football coach in the country — that's his ex-wife saying so. He strives to move up a league both on the pitch and in his personal life, but he tends toward self-destruction and can't find his place. His demands and behavior drive children to leave the team, and his son Shahar is not always willing to open the door to him. When Shahar pulls away, Carlos changes his approach, but he still feels foreign in his surroundings. His ailing mother can't give him what he needs either, and he goes to the protests against the judicial reform mainly to find love. But there is a love almost no one knows about. Will he find a way to make a substantive change and feel like he belongs?
