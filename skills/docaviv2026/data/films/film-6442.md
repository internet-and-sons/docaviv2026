## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Daniel Gad steps for the first time into the director and creator's chair, with a big question and a song in his heart, setting out on a personal journey alongside his father, a former player and icon at the football club. Two adjacent neighborhoods in the city of Rehovot: Sha'arayim, founded in 1911 by immigrants from the Hayden district of Yemen, and Marmorek, founded by immigrants from the Sharab district. Each has its own football team, founded at the end of the 1940s, with rich traditions and a years-long rivalry so deep that even in the cemetery a wall separates the communities. Maccabi Sha'arayim and Hapoel Marmorek — the Rehovot derby. Now a new stadium is being built in the city. Will the dream unite the neighborhoods or divide them forever?
