## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
For over twenty years, the Israeli Kneller family owned a motel in the U.S. called "Town Line," where the family also lived. The 19-room motel hosted varied and colorful guests. The motel's rooms and walls are too silent to tell what happened between them — an absurd telenovela of infidelities, suicides, forbidden relationships, drug deals and disappearances. The Kneller family had to deal with everything happening around them, and as time passed the path of the family's life began to resemble the chaotic, colorful world that existed inside the motel's rooms. A film by Arik Kneller.
