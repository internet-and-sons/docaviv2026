## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Pasolini sets out on a journey through Israel and Jordan in the footsteps of New Testament sites, looking for locations for his film about Jesus, but discovers a landscape that doesn't match the image he had imagined in advance. From the search materials a kind of draft is created — a personal documentary in which the journey itself becomes the central subject. The narration, at times enthusiastic and at times disappointed, clashes with the attractive images and undermines them. Is the failure spontaneous, or is it an essential part of the process, or perhaps even a result known in advance? This is a film about searching, but mainly about the gap between vision and reality, and the moment when disenchantment itself becomes a creative act. Pasolini will eventually film his Jesus film in Italy, but the disappointment from Israel becomes here a singular theological and cinematic move.
