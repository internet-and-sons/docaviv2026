## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
In the last decade it seems reality has fulfilled what was, until recently, science fiction: autopilot, autonomous vehicles, and the promise that we will at last be done with accidents caused by human error. Millions wanted a piece of the progress pie, and the profit went to the electric car company "Tesla" and the man behind it, Elon Musk, who quickly transformed from utopian prophet to false messiah. The choice to open the film not with trumpet calls from Silicon Valley but with the results of a tragic accident that occurred at roughly the same time signals the eye-opening direction of the investigation at its center. Through testimony from whistleblowers, former senior employees and journalists, and unequivocal data and footage, an organizational culture is exposed in which marketing and stock issuance precede technological maturity. Beyond the safety question, the film sketches a disturbing mental portrait of the world's richest man, who forcefully tramples any regulation or obstacle to his vision of the future. And that is the question hovering above: to whom does that future really belong, and who will profit from it?
