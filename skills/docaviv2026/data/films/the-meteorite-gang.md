A director at the height of a midlife crisis runs away to Alaska. In the far north of the American continent he encounters a family whose backyard contains an old bus — inside which sits an enormous rock that, they claim, is a precious meteorite. An unexpected alliance is formed between the director and Dave, the rock's owner — two men who refuse to give up the hope that something big is still in store for them. As the search for the truth continues, the line between documentation and fiction blurs.
