## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
A film set at Mitzpeh Sderot, an observation point that for years served as an early-warning post and has since become a charged memorial site. Today, a coin-operated telescope is mounted there, aimed directly at Gaza. Visiting it are children, soldiers, Taglit groups, foreign tourists and families. Through the act of payment, clearance and peering, the film captures their immediate response, and documents a cross-section of Israeli and Jewish society confronting destruction in real time. The film observes the observation of others' suffering, and invites the viewer to deal with a complex reality and with the hidden sides of the human soul.
