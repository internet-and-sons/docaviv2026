## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
How many times have you heard an ordinary person recite conspiracy slogans or echo unfounded talk that promotes chaos, and imagined yourself proving how wrong they were? Director Robin Kvapil began his film with an unusual help-wanted ad: "Think the war in Ukraine is a hoax? Come be a hero in a new film." From dozens of candidates, three Czech citizens were selected who hold pro-Putin sympathies and believe the Western media — which is also, in their view, too liberal and too green — is enlisted on Ukraine's behalf. To crack their rigid ideological armor, perhaps even bring them back into the fold of facts, Kvapil takes them on a shocking trip up to the front line, where they can witness up close and with their own eyes what it means to live amid destruction and existential threat. The experiment leads to painful encounters and to a need to deal with its shocking results. A fascinating, deeply relevant human document of the post-truth age, raising the sharpest of questions: do facts still matter? Screenings of this film are made possible by the generous support of the Czech Center, Tel Aviv.
