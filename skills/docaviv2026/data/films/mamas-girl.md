## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
A dramatic, intimate real-time documentation of the relationship between a mother and her daughter under extreme conditions. The uncompromising attempts of Liza, a pensioner and single mother, to save the life of Tal — a transgender woman in a state of rapid deterioration, working in prostitution and addicted to drugs — lead to a dead end. But what is visible to the eye is not the whole story, and what gradually emerges changes the picture of reality and requires viewers to examine their own positions on the boundaries of parenthood and motherhood.
