## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
On a round circus stage, beneath a spotlight illuminating his passion into the distance, a dancer does as his spirit wills with his body. He moves and leaps, overcoming gravity, while the troupe around him is swept after him on invisible strings. Jorge Donn did not just dance — he ruled. With rare archival materials, the rhythmic documentary weaves the story of the spectacular Argentine dancer, who began his career as a 16-year-old boy unwilling to take no for an answer from Maurice Béjart's ballet company, and left everything to follow love. The unique bond between Donn and Béjart exceeded the bounds of director and performer; they were lovers, creator and material, with the dancer serving as muse for the choreographer's works. A portrait of a trailblazing artist in the world of dance who won fame for his hypnotic appearance as the solo dancer of "Boléro" in Claude Lelouch's film. Even as his body betrayed him more and more, he refused to stop, believing there is nothing in life but movement.
