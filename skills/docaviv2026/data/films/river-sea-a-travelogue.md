## תקציר
A birth in a pasture, a thunderstorm over the sea, a girl dancing in the water and an ultra-Orthodox man in a wheelchair praying on the shore. An olive harvest in a family grove turns, in an instant, into a closed military zone, and a Chinese worker spends the Sabbath fishing. These moments and others from the past year, within a local, human, and chaotic territory, will ultimately gather into a single work: “River/Sea: A Travelogue.”
