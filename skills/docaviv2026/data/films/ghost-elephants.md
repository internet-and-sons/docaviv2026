## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
In his new film, faithful to his unique way of observing nature and human nature, Werner Herzog accompanies biologist Steve Boyes, a researcher driven by a years-long obsession to find a giant, mysterious and elusive elephant species, in the heart of a wild forested plateau in Angola. Boyes hunts for DNA samples of the jumbo creatures in an attempt to prove that the fantasy exists in reality, with help from the most skilled trackers. The physical hunt for ghost elephants is also a spiritual journey with a dreamlike quality, and an opportunity for Herzog to engage with memories of the pre–civil war past, which slaughtered indiscriminately every creature in its way, and with the prophetic memories of a time that may already have been and may yet come. The camera work, which lingers on local customs, the beautiful images that imagine the herd's journey, and the soundtrack — built around Herzog's so-recognizable narrated voice — converge into a mesmerizing work told through the subjective filter of a cinematic master, who proves in practice that there is no limit to human curiosity.
