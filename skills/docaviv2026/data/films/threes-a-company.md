## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Nava, Miki and Ilan have run an unusual family unit for five decades. The three live together in the same building and jointly raise May, their adopted daughter. Alongside their personal lives, they also founded and still run an iconic, kicking cultural institution — the Tmuna theater. Now, an unavoidable move from the crumbling building on Shontzino Street in Tel Aviv's Garages quarter to an alienating high-tech area threatens the future of the theater's character and shakes the trio. Through intimate conversations and archival material, the three tell about the personal home they built and the professional home that is their life's work, and wonder: will it be possible to preserve the theater's spirit even in the new building?
