## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Esther Ofarim, one of the greatest voices to come out of Israel, won dizzying success — and then, almost in a single day, disappeared. For decades she has lived in her modest Hamburg apartment, far from the Middle Eastern bustle. Through phone calls between Shaked, the film's director in Tel Aviv, and Esther in Germany, the geographic and emotional distance between the two is laid out. This is a journey between places, identities and times, from which emerges the delicate, elusive figure of an artist who is a symbol and distillation of Israeliness, an essence she both rejects and embraces.
