## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
A reservist experiences mounting fracture in the face of the continuing war whose end is not in sight. From an inside view, the film follows the quiet voice of a group of reservists who answered the call to come and found themselves groaning under the public burden. Despite the injustice, despite the disconnection from life, despite the despair and exhaustion. A personal and exposed documentation from inside the war, from October 7 to these very days.
