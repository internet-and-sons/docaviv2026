## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
A Christmas tale that will make you long for the warmth of a burning hearth in the middle of a heatwave. It opens with a mystery that has already become family myth: for more than two decades, an anonymous package of gifts has been arriving on the doorstep of the Sandness couple in northern Norway with the standard inscription "Merry Christmas from all of us in Arendal." After years of guesses and theories, the two young grandchildren — nine-year-old Adele and six-year-old Brage — decide to embark on a Christmas-eve adventure spanning the country to solve the mystery once and for all. What begins as an amusing search, including interrogating relatives and neighbors, analyzing old dedication notes and chasing security-camera footage, takes a deep and moving turn. Director Cilje Jakobsen, who is also the mother of the young detectives, serves up a tasty and precise cinematic dish that successfully balances heart-warming humor, real suspense and moments of human grace.
