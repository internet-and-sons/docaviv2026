## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
An animated mixed-media documentary observing the life of an elderly couple in their crowded apartment. The film follows the quiet, repetitive routine of their day and gradually peels back the layers of objects filling their home. During an innocent English lesson, a turning point arrives, and a celebration rich in colors and textures from their shared world is revealed to us.
