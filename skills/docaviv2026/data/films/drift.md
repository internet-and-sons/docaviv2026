## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
The images of Isaac Wright, known as "Drift," hanging without ropes on the edges of skyscrapers, are almost impossible to look at. Wright, a photographer, climber and artist built in the social media era, has made rooftops and the abysses gaping between them the raw material of his work, the photography itself always tangled with trespass into forbidden spaces and a powerful pull toward danger. Wright, an army veteran suffering from PTSD, found quiet in a place that demands inner peace as a survival tool. But his free flight is blocked by the arms of the law, which targeted him for his unconventional lifestyle and no less for the color of his skin. As he sophisticatedly navigates between documenting the chilling climbing process, the legal-suspense drama playing out on the ground and his ascent as a successful artist, "Drift" derives its power not only from the extreme experience Wright's body cam supplies but also from his insights about life, which kept bringing him down even as he chose to keep aiming up.
