## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
The story of a volcano on the island of Guadeloupe that was about to erupt in 1976. Despite the order to evacuate the island, one man refused to leave. When Werner Herzog read about him in the newspaper, he hurried to pack a camera crew and fly to the Caribbean. Up the mountain he met the object of his search: a stubborn man of the kind that challenges the limits of human behavior.
