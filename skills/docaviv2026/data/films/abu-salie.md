## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Twenty years after filming "Souvenirs," Shahar lives in Amsterdam and is raising two children, a Yemenite-Dutch mix, just like the descendants they once sought for his late father, who so wanted grandchildren. Shahar makes a living from a falafel food truck considered the best in the city, and he employs refugees from all over the world. His teenage son Saleh has a sharp tongue and plenty of criticism. He is embarrassed that Shahar works in the market and pressures him to open a respectable restaurant. Shahar also dreams of a restaurant, but gives up on the dream when he realizes he would have to fire the workers. The surprising solution comes precisely from Saleh, who discovers what truly matters in life.
