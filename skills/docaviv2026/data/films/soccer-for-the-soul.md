## Synopsis (EN) *(agent-authored translation; Docaviv doesn't publish EN descriptions)*
Even though they had scattered across the country, even though they had lost friends and family, even though their friends had been kidnapped to Gaza — a few days after October 7, the players of "Shu'alei Kfar Aza," the football team who survived the terrible day, decided to keep playing football. They understood that the framework, the chase after the rolling ball and the arguments with the referee would protect them, even if only for ninety minutes. When the offer came to return and play in a tournament of the kibbutzim around Gaza, they didn't know what to choose — was it right to play "in memory of" (the murdered) and "for the sake of" (the hostages), or to refuse to return to routine?
